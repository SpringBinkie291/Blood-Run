Why do RBCs sickle?

HbS PATHWAY

Deoxygenation can drive HbS polymerization, increasing red-cell rigidity and promoting sickling.

🫁Low O₂Deoxygenation
🧬HbS PolymersHemoglobin aggregates
🌙SicklingRigid cell shape
⚠️Impaired FlowVaso-occlusion

In sickle cell disease, deoxygenated HbS can polymerize. This contributes to rigid, sickle-shaped RBCs that can obstruct small vessels and impair oxygen delivery.
