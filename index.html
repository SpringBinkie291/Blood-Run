Why do RBCs sickle?

HbS → deoxygenation → polymerization → sickling → impaired flow.

In sickle cell disease, deoxygenated HbS can polymerize. This contributes to rigid, sickle-shaped RBCs that can obstruct small vessels and impair oxygen delivery.
